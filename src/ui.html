Landing Page Grader

Paste any URL. It fetches the live page, works out who the page is for, and scores it for that audience. Every point traces back to something on the page.

    The score is computed by a fixed rubric from what is in the page's HTML and from PageSpeed Insights. No LLM is involved in scoring. Forms and content that only appear after JavaScript runs can't be seen, and those signals are marked "n/a" rather than guessed.
